## Full description

You still love the project. You just lost your place.

Unpause helps you return to the things you make: the half-sewn tote, the watercolor on your desk, the weekend build waiting in the garage.

Before you stop, leave yourself a handoff. Save a photo, what you finished, one small next step, and where the materials live. When a little free time opens up, everything is ready to come back to.

MAKE THE MOST OF A SMALL WINDOW

Choose 10, 25, or 45 minutes and the energy you have. Find projects with a next step that fits. See blockers clearly, so you can decide what is ready for today.

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF

Your resume card brings together the latest photo, stopping point, next action, and materials. Less hunting through scattered notes. More context in one place.

LEAVE A WAY BACK

Start an optional session timer. When you stop, save a new checkpoint. Photos and notes build a history you can revisit. Finish a project when it feels finished.

A CALMER PLACE FOR YOUR HOBBIES

No streaks to protect. No overdue projects. Set a device reminder if you want a gentle invitation to return. Reminders depend on notification permission and your device settings.

START FREE

Keep three unfinished projects free, with session history and completed projects. Studio unlocks unlimited unfinished projects with a one-time purchase. Your existing memories stay readable. Restore purchases from the purchasing store account.

YOUR PROJECTS, ON YOUR DEVICE

The core app works without signup. Export and import a backup when you want. An optional account does not synchronize project data. Back up before switching devices or uninstalling the app. Backups embed your project photos. A size budget prevents new content from exceeding portable-backup limits; device storage limits also apply.

Unpause is a project-memory tool. You provide the craft instructions and decide what to make next.

Leave yourself a way back.
